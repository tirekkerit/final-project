Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is one of the most famous and successful footballers of all time. Born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal, he began his professional career at Sporting CP before gaining worldwide attention at Manchester United. His speed, athletic ability, and goal scoring made him one of the sport's biggest stars.

Ronaldo became a legend at Real Madrid, where he won multiple trophies and broke numerous scoring records. He later played for Juventus and returned to Manchester United before joining Al-Nassr. At the international level, he helped Portugal win Euro 2016 and became the country's all-time leading scorer.

Playing Style

Ronaldo's playing style has evolved throughout his career. Early on, he was known for his stepovers, dribbling, and creativity on the wing. Later, he became a powerful striker recognized for his finishing, movement, speed, strength, and incredible heading ability.
