Luka Modrić

Luka Modrić is one of the greatest midfielders in football history. Born on September 9, 1985, in Zadar, Croatia, he overcame a difficult childhood to become a world-class player. His technical ability, intelligence, and calmness under pressure have made him one of the most respected players of his generation.

Modrić became a legend at Real Madrid, where he helped the club win numerous trophies, including multiple UEFA Champions League titles. In 2018, he led Croatia to their first FIFA World Cup final and won the Ballon d'Or, becoming the first player other than Lionel Messi or Cristiano Ronaldo to win the award since 2007. After 13 seasons in Madrid, he joined AC Milan in 2025, continuing his career in Serie A into his forties.

Modrić is known for his exceptional passing, vision, and ability to control the rhythm of a match. His close control, quick decision-making, and creativity allow him to escape pressure and create opportunities. Despite his smaller frame, his intelligence and technique make him one of football's greatest midfielders.
