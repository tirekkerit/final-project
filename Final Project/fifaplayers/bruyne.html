Kevin De Bruyne

Kevin De Bruyne is considered one of the best midfielders of his generation. Born on June 28, 1991, in Drongen, Belgium, he developed his skills at Genk before making a name for himself in European football. His vision, passing ability, and intelligence have made him one of the most creative players in modern football.

After successful spells with Wolfsburg and Chelsea, De Bruyne became a superstar at Manchester City. He helped lead the club to multiple Premier League titles and major trophies while becoming known as one of the world's greatest playmakers. His contract with City expired in the summer of 2024, and he moved to Napoli in 2025, where he helped the Italian club win the Serie A title in his first season. For Belgium, he has been a key figure during their golden generation.
