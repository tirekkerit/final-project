Vinícius Jr.

Vinícius Jr. is one of the most exciting young attackers in world football. Born on July 12, 2000, in São Gonçalo, Brazil, he developed his skills at Flamengo before moving to Real Madrid as a teenager. His explosive speed, creativity, and confidence have made him one of football's biggest stars.

After adapting to European football, Vinícius became a key player for Real Madrid. He has played an important role in major victories, including the 2022 UEFA Champions League final where he scored the winning goal. For Brazil, he continues to be one of the team's most important attacking players.
