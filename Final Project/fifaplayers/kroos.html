Toni Kroos

Toni Kroos is one of the most technically gifted midfielders of his generation. Born on January 4, 1990, in Greifswald, Germany, he developed through Bayern Munich's youth system before becoming known for his incredible passing, composure, and ability to control the pace of a match.

Kroos became a legend at Real Madrid, where he formed one of the greatest midfield partnerships in football history. Alongside Luka Modrić and Casemiro, he helped the club win multiple Champions League titles. With Germany, Kroos played a major role in their 2014 FIFA World Cup victory.

Playing Style

Kroos is known for his exceptional passing accuracy, calm decision-making, and ability to control games from midfield. Rather than relying on speed, he uses intelligence, positioning, and technique to dictate the flow of matches and create opportunities for his teammates.
