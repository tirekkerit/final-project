Pedri

Pedri is one of the most talented young midfielders in world football. Born on November 25, 2002, in Tegueste, Spain, he developed his skills at Las Palmas before joining FC Barcelona as a teenager. His vision, creativity, and ability to read the game have made him one of Spain's most exciting players.

Since joining Barcelona, Pedri has become a key part of the team's midfield. He has been praised for his maturity, technical ability, and calmness under pressure despite his young age. With Spain, he has represented the country at major international tournaments and continues to be an important player for the national team.
