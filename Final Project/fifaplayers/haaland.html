Erling Haaland

Erling Haaland is one of the most dominant goal scorers in modern football. Born on July 21, 2000, in Leeds, England, and raised in Norway, he developed his skills at Bryne and Molde before becoming a European superstar. His strength, speed, and incredible finishing ability have made him one of the most feared strikers in the world.

Haaland gained international attention at Red Bull Salzburg before becoming a star at Borussia Dortmund. He later joined Manchester City, where he continued breaking scoring records and helped the club win major trophies. For Norway, he has become the centerpiece of the national team's attack.
